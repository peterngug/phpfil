Why Us

The Student Performance Evaluation System is a system that is aimed at challenging students to keep working hard by investing more time in their studies by predicting their grades based on the time they invest in their studies.

Mission

To provide a platform that will help students to track their study hours and predict their grades based on the time they invest in their studies.

Vision

To be the leading platform that helps students to track their study hours and predict their grades based on the time they invest in their studies.
